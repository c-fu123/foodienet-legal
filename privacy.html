Information we collect

Required to use the Service unless marked optional:

  • Account information — name, email address, and phone number, collected via our authentication provider (Clerk) when you sign up with Apple, Google, or email.
  • Profile information — your Instagram and/or TikTok handle, city, country, postcode, profile photo, and portfolio video links, which you provide during onboarding. At least one social handle is required; without it, we can't match you to offers.
  • Approximate location — we send your postcode, city, and country to Google's Geocoding API and store back only the resulting coordinates (precise to roughly 10cm), used to show you nearby restaurant offers and filter by distance. We don't collect your device's GPS location, and we don't store the raw address or Google's full response — only the coordinates.
  • Collaboration content — your applications, proof-of-content submissions (links to your published posts), and any review screenshot you upload.
  • Messages — messages you send within the app go to FoodieNet's team, not directly to a restaurant or another creator; we store the message content.
  • Reports — if you report a message or a listing, we store the report content, a copy of what you reported, and your account so our team can review it.
  • Push notification token (optional) — only if you grant notification permission, so we can send you collaboration updates.
  • Diagnostic data — crash and error reports (via Sentry on the mobile app and API) to help us fix bugs.   • Payment information — we don't currently collect or process any payment information. This will be updated if that changes.

How we use your information, and our legal basis

  • To create and manage your account, and verify you're eligible to use the Service — necessary to perform our contract with you.
  • To match you with relevant nearby restaurant offers and process your applications — necessary to perform our contract with you.
  • To pass your relevant details (e.g. handle and submitted proof) to the restaurant you're collaborating with — necessary to perform our contract with you.
  • To send you transactional emails and push notifications about your account and collaborations — necessary to perform our contract with you.
  • To review reported content and enforce our Terms — our legitimate interest in keeping the Service safe and trustworthy, balanced against your rights.
  • To diagnose and fix technical issues — our legitimate interest in keeping the Service reliable.

We don't currently rely on your consent for any of the above — where we do ask for consent (for example, optional marketing messages, if introduced), we'll tell you at the time and you can withdraw it at any point by contacting us or using the relevant in-app setting.

Who can see your information

Your profile and applications are never publicly visible — they're only seen by FoodieNet's own team (restaurants don't currently have their own login to the Service; FoodieNet's team manages offers and reviews applications/proof on their behalf) and, for a collaboration you've applied to, the restaurant you're collaborating with.

Account deletion — what actually happens

You can delete your own account at any time from Profile → Delete account in the app. This immediately clears your name, phone number, social handles, location, profile photo, and portfolio links from your account, and cancels any active collaborations.

To be accurate about what this doesn't do: messages you've sent, your proof submissions (including any uploaded screenshot), and any reports involving your account are not deleted or anonymised — they're kept as a record, still linked to your (otherwise cleared) account, so FoodieNet can resolve disputes and maintain accurate collaboration records. [PENDING — justified retention periods for these categories, and whether/when they should be deleted or genuinely anonymised instead — not yet decided; this policy will be updated once that's resolved]. Files you've uploaded (e.g. a profile photo or proof screenshot) are also not currently deleted from our storage provider when you delete your account — we're addressing this.

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a collaboration obligation you'd already completed or that was still owed at the time of deletion.
